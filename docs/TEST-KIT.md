# Cubby: 2-minute test kit

For a home daycare provider, nanny or parent. Use only the fictional demo day; never enter real children.

**Send this:**
> Hi! I'm testing a tiny app for home daycare providers. It turns quick notes into parent daily sheets and the licensing log, and it flags anything an inspector would cite. It takes 2 minutes and uses only made-up kids. Link: <APP_URL>. Feedback form at the end: <APP_URL>/feedback.html. Thank you!

## Script (2 minutes)

1. **(20 s)** Open the link on your phone. Without scrolling, say what you think the app does. *(We're checking whether the first screen explains itself.)*
2. **(30 s)** Tap **To fix**. Read the first two items. Is each one a real problem you'd want to know about before an inspector or a parent finds it? Does the quoted note make clear why it was flagged?
3. **(20 s)** On Theo's bump, tap **Open pre-filled report**. What's missing, and what would you change?
4. **(30 s)** Tap **Parents → Ava**. Pick Spanish (or a language your families use) and tap **Write in Spanish**. Would a parent be happy to get this? If you read Spanish, does it say anything the log doesn't?
5. **(20 s)** In the bottom bar, type or say a note about your own day with made-up names. For example: "Mila napped 12:30 to 2, checked her at 12:45". Does it land where you expected?
6. Open **/feedback.html** and answer 5 quick questions.

## What we're watching for (from public reviews and inspection reports; see research/FORUMS.md)

- Can sheets be finished on a phone during nap time? (Providers describe doing paper sheets at nap time from memory.)
- Sleep checks: California inspection reports cite missing 15-minute check times. Does the gap flag make sense?
- Translation safety: a public app review describes a Spanish machine translation that turned a child being "out" sick into a child who had "died". Cubby never re-words incident or medicine lines and keeps the English facts under any translation. Does that feel safe enough?
- Price: providers complain about per-child pricing found after a trial. What would they pay?

## Rules for reporting results

- Quote testers only with their permission, and never by name unless they say so.
- If nobody tests it, the post says so. We don't write a "first user" section without one.
