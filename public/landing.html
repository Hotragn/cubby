The problem

One adult, a room of toddlers, and two kinds of paperwork.

A home daycare provider is often alone with 4 to 6 children all day.

Every parent wants a daily report, and the licensing inspector wants exact logs: sign-ins, naps and sleep checks, medicine, injuries.

Most of it gets written from memory at nap time, and inspectors cite what's missing, like a sleep log without the time of each 15-minute check.

How it works

You jot it. Cubby logs it, checks it and writes it up.

Private by design

Children's records stay with you.

  • On your device. Your children, notes and photos are saved in your browser, not on our server.
  • An open AI model you can run yourself. Cubby uses Gemma, an open model, and can run it on your own computer, so notes never leave it.
  • The AI only reads. Plain code decides what's a problem, and every warning shows the note and the rule behind it.
On 14 made-up test days with 37 planted problems, Cubby caught all 37, with 4 false alarms. The same AI model on its own made 72 false alarms. Read the full report.
